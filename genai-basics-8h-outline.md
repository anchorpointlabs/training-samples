# GenAI basics: 8 h outline

For staff who have used ChatGPT casually and never been taught. By the end they can judge when to trust the output and when to check it.

| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. What these tools actually are | 60 min | Prediction machines, not databases. Why answers are fluent but sometimes wrong. Live demo: a confident wrong answer, caught. |
| 2. Prompting that works | 90 min | CO-STAR structure. One task, three prompts, compare. |
| 3. Judging the output | 90 min | The check list: facts, confidentiality, fit. Spot-the-error exercise on a realistic document. |
| 4. Everyday uses in your company | 90 min | Each learner brings one real task. Draft, check, improve in pairs. |
| 5. The Cockpit Rule | 60 min | Autopilot / Collaboration / Manual. Sorting the company's own tasks into the three modes. |
| 6. Rules and limits | 60 min | What never goes into an AI. The company's AI-use policy, read and tested. |
| 7. Capstone | 30 min | Each learner runs their real task end to end and presents what they checked. |
