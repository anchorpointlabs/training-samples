# Copilot — 8 h outline

For Microsoft 365 shops. By the end staff can use Copilot inside their daily tools and know where it earns its cost.

| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Where Copilot lives | 60 min | Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, Outlook. What it can read and what it cannot. |
| 2. Prompting inside documents | 90 min | Summarise, rewrite, extract action items from real documents. |
| 3. Excel and data | 90 min | Ask questions of a real spreadsheet. Formula help, trend questions, chart requests. |
| 4. Meetings and email | 90 min | Teams recap, email drafting, turning a thread into a task list. |
| 5. Boundaries and permissions | 60 min | What Copilot indexes, what it cannot see, and what that means for confidential work. |
| 6. Your workflows | 60 min | Each learner maps their week: which of their real tasks fit Copilot, which do not. |
| 7. Capstone | 30 min | Each learner runs one mapped task live and shows the before/after. |
