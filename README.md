# Training Samples

Public samples of Anchor Point Labs' training menu: four course outlines, one sample exercise each. The full materials are delivered in training engagements, built on the client's own documents.

No client names, no results, no claims — this is the menu, not the record.

## What training looks like

- Built on the client's own material: their SOPs, their documents, their workflows. Not generic examples.
- Activity before slide: every concept is proven by an exercise the learner runs, not a slide the trainer reads.
- After the training, the exercises stay with the company as working prompts and templates.
